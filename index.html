How a pairing works

Four steps, and money changes hands at step three.

Most collaborations die in the group chat. This is the order we make people work in, because it's the order that survives.

01

Post one beat

Not the whole saga. One scene, up to 400 words, plus the tone you're after.

02

Read the shortlist

Five people whose pages fit your beat. You see their work, rates and turnaround before you write to anyone.

03

Commission a test page

One page, paid up front, held in escrow. Either side can walk after it with no bad blood.

04

Bind the book

Sign a page rate or a rights split from our templates. Meetink takes 7% and stays out of the way.

The terms

Who owns it, who gets paid, who can leave.

The three questions that break collaborations. Answer them on day one, in a template you can read in five minutes.

Rights

Neither of you signs it away

Writers keep the story, artists keep the pages, adaptation money splits by whatever you agreed. Meetink never takes a stake in the work.

Money

Escrow on every page

The fee is held before the artist starts and releases on delivery. No page gets drawn on a promise.

Exit

A kill fee, not a fight

Either side can end a pairing at a chapter break. The half-finished chapter pays at 50% and everyone keeps their files.
